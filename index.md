# Durak Support

Need help with Durak? Email support at:

[[email redacted]](mailto:[email redacted])

Durak Version 1 is a free offline card game. It does not require an account,
does not include ads, does not include in-app purchases, and does not include
online multiplayer.

When contacting support, include:

- your device model;
- iOS or Android version;
- Durak app version;
- what you were doing when the issue happened;
- screenshots or locally exported bug-report text, if you choose to share it.

Privacy Policy: [Durak Privacy Policy](https://savastosic3.github.io/Durak-Privacy-Policy/).
