# Durak Support

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Need help with Durak? Email support at:

<support.durak@gmail.com>

**How long a reply takes.** Durak is made by one person, so please allow up to
**two weeks** for a response. Requests about your personal data — a copy of it,
a correction, or deletion — are answered within 30 days as described in the
Privacy Policy, and usually sooner.

Durak is a free card game with no ads. Offline play needs no account. Online
multiplayer is optional and can be played as a guest or with a free account. It
offers optional cosmetic theme packs as one-time in-app purchases; separate
cosmetic themes can be earned for free through achievements, and purchases never
affect gameplay.

When contacting support, include:

- your device model;
- iOS or Android version;
- Durak app version;
- what you were doing when the issue happened;
- screenshots or locally exported bug-report text, if you choose to share it.

## Online Rating

Rated online games change a public rating calculated on the server from the
result of the match. A game is rated only when it is a public room of two to six
verified accounts with no bots. Private games, games with bots, and games with a
guest at the table are never rated — the room shows **Rated** or **Unranked**
before the game starts, with the reason.

Every player's rating starts at 1000. A win takes points from the player who
finished as the Durak and gives them to everyone else, so a rated match always
balances to zero. A very one-sided win can be worth zero points; that is normal
and the game still counted.

If a rating looks wrong, email support **within one week of the match** with
your username and roughly when it was played. Match records become eligible for
automatic deletion seven days after the server writes them; physical deletion
can occur later. Contact us promptly because those records may no longer be
available when we investigate. A delayed deletion is not an extended support
or record-availability guarantee.

## Restoring Purchases

Durak checks the App Store or Google Play for packs you own each time it starts.
If a pack is still missing after you reinstall Durak or set up a new device,
open the **Store** tab and tap **Restore Purchases**. This re-reads your
purchases and re-unlocks any cosmetic packs you own, using the same store
account you bought them with.

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. For billing or refund requests, use
the App Store or Google Play purchase history for your account. If a restored
pack does not appear, confirm you are signed in to the store account used for
the original purchase, then try Restore Purchases again.

**Purchases restore only on the platform you bought them on.** A pack bought
through the App Store restores on your other Apple devices, and a pack bought
through Google Play restores on your other Android devices — but a purchase
made on one platform cannot be restored on the other. This is deliberate for
this version: Durak keeps a local record of unlocked packs, but has no server
purchase or cross-platform receipt ledger. Apple and Google provide purchase
restoration through their respective stores. If you move to the other platform
you would need to buy the pack again there.

Paid packs are cosmetic only — card art, card backs and the table — so this
never affects how the game plays, what you can do, or your stats and ratings.
A confirmed purchase or restore also unlocks the on-device "Supporting the
Cause" achievement. The free themes and the themes unlocked by achievements are not
purchases and are unaffected by any of this.
